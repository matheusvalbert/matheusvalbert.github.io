GDPR Privacy Policy

Programmer Calculator – by Matheus Valbert

Last updated: 2025-11-26

This GDPR Privacy Policy explains how Programmer Calculator (“we”, “our”, “us”), developed by Matheus Valbert, processes personal data when you use the application within the European Economic Area (EEA).

1. Data We Collect

1.1. No Personal Information Collected

We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information. We do not track users, create profiles, fingerprint devices, or collect analytics.

1.2. Automatically Collected Technical Data

Certain non-identifiable data may be collected automatically through Google Play services, such as:

  • App version
  • Device model and operating system version
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

This information is strictly used to improve app stability and fix bugs. It does not identify you.

2. Advertising & GDPR Consent (IAB TCF 2.x)

Programmer Calculator (free version) uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. For users in the EEA, we comply with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) 2.x.

2.1. Consent Management Platform (CMP)

We use a GDPR-compliant Consent Management Platform (CMP), such as the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), to collect your consent for:

  • Personalized or non-personalized ads
  • Use of cookies or local storage by AdMob
  • Sharing limited ad-related data with certified vendors

2.2. Your Consent Options

You may:

  • Accept personalized ads
  • Opt out and receive non-personalized ads
  • Reject all (ads will remain non-personal and limited)
  • Modify or withdraw your consent at any time

A “Privacy Options” button is available in the app to reopen the GDPR consent form.

2.3. Data Processed by AdMob

AdMob may process non-personally identifiable information such as:

  • Coarse location (approximate region)
  • Device type and interactions with ads
  • Advertisement performance metrics

Learn more at Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Premium Purchases

Premium features (Unlimited word size, Evaluate with Steps, Remove ads) are processed through Google Play Billing.

We do not collect or store payment data. All payment information is handled exclusively by Google.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

We rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent – for advertising purposes via CMP
  • Legitimate Interest – for crash logs & diagnostics
  • Contract – when unlocking premium features

5. Data Retention

We do not store personal data. Crash logs and ad-related data are retained by Google under their own retention schedule.

6. Your GDPR Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data processed by third-party providers
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Request deletion (we don’t store user data ourselves)
  • Restrict or object to data processing

To exercise your rights or ask questions, you may contact us at: matheusvalbert@gmail.com

7. Children’s Privacy

Programmer Calculator is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from minors.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this GDPR Privacy Policy when necessary. Updates will be published on this page.
